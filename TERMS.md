## What Ciela Does

Ciela is a desktop application that connects to your Gmail account to help you understand and clean up your inbox. It reads email metadata locally on your device and does not transmit your email content to any server.

## Your Google Account

You can revoke this access at any time through your Google Account settings at [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com) or by disconnecting within the app.

Ciela requests only the permissions it needs. It will never send email on your behalf.

Ciela is not responsible for emails you choose to trash. Review your selections carefully before confirming.
