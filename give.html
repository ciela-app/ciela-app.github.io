Choose a charity to support

Opens Every.org — search from thousands of verified nonprofits. Ciela never sees your payment details.

01

You choose the cause

Search Every.org's database of verified nonprofits — from local food banks to the Internet Archive to environmental funds. Your values, your decision.

02

Every.org handles everything

Payment, receipt, tax documentation. We don't see your card, we don't process your donation, and we don't take a cut. It goes directly to the charity.

03

We see the signal

Every.org tells us a Ciela user donated — which charity and how much, anonymously. That's it. No account, no tracking, no follow-up.

About Every.org

Every.org is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that makes giving simple and transparent. They vet thousands of charities, process donations fee-free for donors, and provide attribution so mission-driven products like Ciela can see the impact. We chose them because they share the same value we do: no middlemen taking a cut of money meant for good causes.
